How MellowCV works

From blank page to ready‑to‑send.

The workflow is designed around the decisions that matter when you're applying, not around features you have to learn.

1

Build your CV

Add your experience, education and skills. Keep the information yours; let the layout do the organising.

2

Strengthen the writing

Improve weak descriptions, make achievements clearer, and keep important information easy to find.

3

Write the cover letter

Connect your experience to the role instead of sending the same generic letter everywhere.

4

Check and send

Choose your final design, review the result, and download your documents when they're ready.
